# ADR-004: Grants and payouts shown, never subtracted

Status: accepted · 25 Nov 2026

## Context

The Home Caregiving Grant, MediSave Care, CareShield Life, ElderFund and the helper levy concession each need an assessment or a separate application. How they stack on one bill isn't published.

## Decision

Grants and payouts are listed beside the total as "may qualify", with amount, conditions and an official link. They are never taken off any total.

## Consequences

The tool never overstates savings. Users see a higher total than some families will finally pay; the listed grants show where it could come down.
