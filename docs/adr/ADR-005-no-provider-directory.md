# ADR-005: No provider directory

Status: accepted · 25 Nov 2026

## Context

data.gov.sg's "Eldercare Services" layer was last edited in July 2016, and there is no open dataset for Active Ageing Centres, AIC Links, day care or nursing homes. A stale or partial directory would send families to the wrong place.

## Decision

Curate a short "who to call" list: the 9 AIC Links and the 28 polyclinics, all checked against official pages on 25 Nov 2026. Hospitals aren't listed: 8 of the 9 AIC Links are inside hospitals, and the in-hospital branch sends families to the ward social worker. Use the open Family Service Centre and Community Club layers for "nearest". Link out to AIC's own locator for centres and nursing homes. Never rank providers.

## Consequences

The list is small enough to check monthly. Families get first contacts, not a provider catalogue.
