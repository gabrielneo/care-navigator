# ADR-007: Nursing homes as a "from" figure beside real bills

Status: accepted · 25 Nov 2026

## Context

A subsidised nursing home bill depends on assessment, ward type and care needs. Arithmetic on AIC's $4,900 "from" fee gives about $91 a month after payouts, while the lowest real subsidised bill published by Ren Ci is $620.

## Decision

Show nursing homes as a labelled "from" figure: $4,900 × (1 − residential subsidy), marked "subsidised beds at government-funded homes, after assessment". Beside it, show real published bills (Ren Ci, Jul 2026: $620–$3,069). Never show one exact figure, and never rank a nursing home against home care.

## Consequences

Families see the right order of magnitude without a false promise. The emotional weight of the choice stays with the family and their care team.
