Care Navigator: lo-fi wireframes

Mobile frames at 375px, using Mei's scenario. Her mother, 86, is about to be discharged after a fall. She needs help washing and dressing, lives alone on weekdays, and her household income is $1,200 per person (range T0). Figures follow the 1 Jul 2026 rules in docs/product-design-v2.md. Wording comes from specs 08 and 09 and hasn't had a practitioner review yet. Scroll sideways to see every frame.

Not drawn here: questions 3–6 (same pattern as 2a), the old-link banner "Figures updated on {date}", the damaged-link banner, the 404 page, and the desktop layout (the same column, centred, max 640px). For a lo-fi pass, a terracotta outline on a peach fill means selected, sage marks reassurance ("why this fits", the first contact), the deep clay block is the headline figure, and dashed boxes stand for pictures still to be drawn.
