## Which providers publish fees, and what do they publish?

### Takeaway
Only a handful publish usable price lists: Homage, Jaga-Me and Active Global publish full gross hourly/visit rate cards (GST-inclusive); NTUC Health publishes only after-subsidy "from" prices; Allium publishes private nursing-home day rates. St Luke's, Orange Valley and Econ publish nothing. I found no fee pages for Thye Hua Kwan, Lions Befrienders, MWS, Touch, Kampung Senang, Allkin or SASCO in the time available.

### Cited Findings
- Homage (pay-as-you-go): ADL caregiving $28/hr weekday 1-2 hr, $29 weeknight/weekend, $32 public holiday; 3-12 hr $27.50/$28.50/$31.50; ADL + simple nursing $34-$39/hr (1-2 hr) and $33-$38/hr (3+ hr); complex nursing from $80/procedure; prepaid ADL blocks $25/hr (10 hr) down to $23/hr (200 hr); PT/OT $180/session or $670 for 4; speech $200; surcharges $5 for visits under 3 hr and for bookings within 24 hr. Prices stated as GST-inclusive. Article published 20 Aug 2026, re-checked 10 Nov 2026 — [MissLobang](https://www.misslobang.com/article/best-home-care-eldercare-services-singapore-2026) (V-secondary)
- Jaga-Me (all GST-inclusive): caregiving/medical escort weekday day $37.06/hr (<8 hr), $34.88 (8+ hr), $32.70 (12+ hr); weekday night $40.33/$38.15/$35.97; weekend/PH $43.60/$41.42/$39.24; nurse caregiving $49.05–$55.59/hr (<8 hr); nursing procedure $130.80–$218 weekday day, $174.40–$261.60 nights/weekends; doctor house call $381.50 (office hours) / $523.20; transport $32.70 per visit — [MissLobang](https://www.misslobang.com/article/best-home-care-eldercare-services-singapore-2026) (V-secondary). Jaga-Me's own help-centre price chart says prices apply from 1 Jan 2024 and include 9% GST — [Jaga-Me help centre](https://jaga-mesupport.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/8022777176857-What-are-the-prices-of-Jaga-Me-s-services) (S; fetch returned 403)
- NTUC Health (after-subsidy "from" prices): HPC+ personal care from $4.80/hr; 4-hr shift from $18.50; housekeeping from $6.30/hr (max 7 subsidised hr/month); home medical from $22.55/visit; home nursing from $10.95/visit; home therapy from $12.70/visit; one-time admin fee $60 before GST; nursing home $134–$178/day — [MissLobang](https://www.misslobang.com/article/best-home-care-eldercare-services-singapore-2026) (V-secondary). Search snippet of NTUC's own page showed home nursing from $8.20/visit + $60 admin fee — [NTUC Health home nursing](https://ntuchealth.sg/home-nursing/) (S; conflicts with $10.95, likely a different date)
- Active Global: private nursing from $27.50/hr; single clinical procedure visit $125.20; home medical $270–$420 per house call by time slot; home PT first session $160 — [MissLobang](https://www.misslobang.com/article/best-home-care-eldercare-services-singapore-2026) (V-secondary)
- Allium Healthcare (private nursing home, before GST): $250–$705/day for stays of 2+ months; one-week stays $340 (Classic Companion) to $800–$830 (Executive Single) per day — [MissLobang](https://www.misslobang.com/article/best-home-care-eldercare-services-singapore-2026) (V-secondary)
- St Luke's ElderCare, Orange Valley and Econ Healthcare: no fees published — [MissLobang](https://www.misslobang.com/article/best-home-care-eldercare-services-singapore-2026) (V-secondary)
- Ren Ci "Financial Charges" page for its senior care centres returned 404 on 24 Nov 2026 — [Ren Ci](https://www.renci.org.sg/Quick-Information/Senior-Care-Centres/Financial-Charges)

### Inferences
- Private providers' gross hourly home-care rates cluster at $25–$44/hr (personal care) and $33–$56/hr (nurse-level care), so a typical gross figure of ~$30/hr for personal care and ~$62–$130 per nursing visit/procedure is defensible.
- Subsidised (VWO) providers mostly publish only after-subsidy floors, which cannot seed a gross price column.

### Gaps
- Thye Hua Kwan, Lions Befrienders, MWS, Touch, Kampung Senang, Allkin, SASCO: not checked individually; no fee pages surfaced in searches.
- Jaga-Me and ask.gov.sg pages returned 403, so Jaga-Me figures rely on MissLobang's transcription.

## What does AIC publish as "starting from" prices?

### Takeaway
AIC publishes gross "starts from" prices on only some service pages: day care $55/session, dementia day care $63/session, home nursing $62/visit, home personal care $25/hour. Transport is "additional cost" with no figure. Pages carry no update date (footer (c) 2026; FAQ refers to 2026 subsidy levels).

### Cited Findings
- Day care from $55 per session, dementia day care from $63 per session, both before subsidies; transport between centre and home at additional cost — [AIC Day Care](https://www.aic.sg/care-services/day-care) (V-primary, fetched 24 Nov 2026)
- Home nursing from $62 per visit before subsidies — [AIC Home Nursing](https://aic.sg/care-services/home-nursing) (V-primary)
- Home personal care from $25 per hour before subsidies; bookable in 4-hour blocks (no price) — [AIC Home Personal Care](https://www.aic.sg/care-services/home-personal-care) (V-primary)
- An aggregator (citing AIC) lists: meals on wheels $4.90–$7/meal; medical escort & transport $40–$150/round trip; home medical from $180/visit; home therapy from $125/visit; home nursing from $80/visit; home personal care from $24.50/hr; day care $945–$1,430/month; dementia day care $1,260–$1,575/month; active rehab $70–$102/session; maintenance rehab $52–$65/session; centre-based nursing $24–$32/service; nursing home respite $100–$150/day; IHDC $1,100–$2,200/month. Updated 13 May 2026 — [thebestsingapore](https://thebestsingapore.com/?p=34309) (V-secondary). Its nursing ($80) and HPC ($24.50) figures conflict with AIC's current $62 and $25, suggesting they come from older AIC pages.
- A government Q&A snippet cites "start from $23 per session" for day care — [ask.gov.sg AIC](https://ask.gov.sg/aic/questions/cls1kszwh00eu1codi486l7qg) (S; likely an after-subsidy or older figure, unverified)

### Inferences
- Use AIC's own figures as the "low" for gross prices; use aggregator ranges for services AIC no longer prices (meals, escort, rehab, respite) and flag them lower confidence.

### Gaps
- No AIC "from" price found for meals on wheels, medical escort/transport, day care transport, home therapy, centre-based rehab, respite or Active Ageing Centres. AIC GST treatment not stated on any page.

## Typical number of sessions/visits per week

### Takeaway
Little direct data. The only explicit assumption found is MOH's monthly day care estimate ($945–$1,430/month) assuming attendance every weekday.

### Cited Findings
- Day care estimated at $945–$1,430 a month before subsidies, assuming daily weekday attendance, excluding transport — [ask.gov.sg MOH](https://ask.gov.sg/moh/questions/cm2sgceyz006n46yze4zp4lia) (S; page returned 403)
- NTUC Health HPC+ housekeeping capped at 7 subsidised hours/month — [MissLobang](https://www.misslobang.com/article/best-home-care-eldercare-services-singapore-2026) (V-secondary)

### Inferences
- $945/month ÷ ~21.7 weekdays ≈ $43.50/session, below AIC's $55 floor, so the MOH monthly figure likely predates the current $55 price or uses a different basis. Do not combine them without noting this.
- Estimator defaults (e.g. day care 3x/week, home nursing 1–2x/week) would be design assumptions, not sourced data.

### Gaps
- No published utilisation data (average day-care days/week, home-care visits/week) found.

## MDW: salary, levy, concession, insurance, agency, grants

### Takeaway
Levy is $300/month (first MDW) or $450 (subsequent), cut to $60 with a concession (care recipient aged 67+, child under 16, or person with disabilities needing help with 1+ ADL). Salary is not regulated; sources put it at ~$450–$850+, with eldercare caregivers via agency $600–$1,000. The Home Caregiving Grant rose to up to $600/month from April 2026.

### Cited Findings
- Concession levy $60/month; one MDW per eligible person, max two per household; eligibility: SC child under 16, SC (or PR with SC link) aged 67+, or PWD needing help with at least one ADL with AIC recommendation letter. Page last updated 7 Jul 2026 — [MOM levy concession](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/foreign-domestic-worker-levy/levy-concession) (V-primary)
- Standard levy $300/month first MDW, $450 subsequent — [MOM PQ reply 8 Apr 2025](https://www.mom.gov.sg/newsroom/parliament-questions-and-replies/2025/0408-oral-answer-to-pq-on-levy-reduction-for-1st-mdw) (S)
- MOM has no plans to lower the age-67 threshold for the elderly concession (May 2026 PQ) — [SingSaver](https://www.singsaver.com.sg/insurance/blog/mom-maid-levy-concessions-household-budgeting-guide) (S; official reply not read) — [MOM PQ 5 May 2026](https://www.mom.gov.sg/newsroom/parliament-questions-and-replies/2026/0505-written-answer-to-pq-on-on-levy-concessions-for-elderly)
- Active Global live-in caregiver salary $600–$1,000/month; placement fee $2,725 (Myanmar/Sri Lanka/India), $2,875 (Indonesia), $3,161 (Philippines), $3,324.50 (Advance Placement), $3,597 (paediatric), all incl. GST; work permit application/issuance $35 each; pre-employment medical $38; insurance (26 months) from $556.90; settling-in programme $75; levy $300 ($60 under schemes) — [MissLobang](https://www.misslobang.com/article/best-home-care-eldercare-services-singapore-2026) (V-secondary)
- No statutory minimum wage for MDWs; baseline ~$500–$850+/month by nationality and experience — [SingSaver](https://www.singsaver.com.sg/page/blog/cost-guide-to-hiring-a-domestic-helper-maid-singapore) (S); home-country minimums ~$450 (Myanmar) to ~$570 (Philippines) — [Income](https://income.com.sg/blog/helper-costs) (S, likely dated)
- Home Caregiving Grant maximum raised from $400 to $600/month and per-capita household income ceiling raised to $4,800 (Budget 2026, effective April 2026); requires help with at least 3 of 6 ADLs, SC/PR — [Singapore Budget 2026 statement](https://singaporebudget.gov.sg/budget-speech/budget-statement/f-nurturing-a-caring-and-inclusive-society) (S); [AIC HCG](https://aic.sg/Financial-Assistance/Home-Caregiving-Grant) (S)

### Inferences
- Monthly MDW running cost for an eldercare household with concession ≈ salary $600–$800 + levy $60 + insurance ~$21/month (556.90/26) + food/lodging (not sourced); without concession add $240.

### Gaps
- No official average MDW salary statistic for 2025–2026.
- MOM's minimum MDW medical insurance coverage requirement not verified in this session.
- Separate "MDW Grant" (older $120/month grant, now folded into HCG per earlier policy) not verified.

## MOH out-of-pocket benchmark (~$110/month)

### Takeaway
MOH says a person with severe disability from a low-income household using "a typical set of care services" at home and in the community, covered by CareShield Life, pays about $110/month out of pocket after the enhancements. MOH does not publish the service mix, frequency or gross cost behind it.

### Cited Findings
- ~$110/month out-of-pocket for a severely disabled, low-income person using typical home/community services, covered by CareShield Life; may also withdraw up to $200/month from MediSave (MediSave Care). Release dated 7 Jul 2026 (Notice Paper No. 936) — [MOH](https://www.moh.gov.sg/newsroom/long-term-care-financing-support-enhanced-to-improve-care-affordability/) (V-primary). The page gives no service list, frequency, gross cost, subsidy % or payout amounts.
- Non-residential LTC subsidies go up to 80%; the 2023 framework tied the top 80% tier to per-capita household income ≤ $600 — [MOH subsidy framework](https://www.moh.gov.sg/managing-expenses/schemes-and-subsidies/subsidy-framework-for-non-residential-long-term-care-services/) (S; tiers may have been revised)
- IHDC pilot: $1,100–$2,300/month before subsidy, ~$300–$600/month at max subsidy — [MOH](https://www.moh.gov.sg/newsroom/more-affordable-intermediate-and-long-term-care-services-to-help-singaporeans-age-in-place) (S; pilot-era, undated)

### Inferences
- The $110 is a net figure after subsidy (likely 80%), HCG and CareShield Life payouts; it is a worked example, not an average, so it can sanity-check an estimator's low-income/high-need output but not calibrate gross prices.

### Gaps
- Assumptions behind $110 (services, frequency) not published on the page read.
- Current (2026) subsidy tier table and CareShield Life payout amounts not verified.
- Active Ageing Centre fees: no source found (commonly described as free for seniors, but unverified here).
